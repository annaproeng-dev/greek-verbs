# Mediterranean Word Sea

Status: idea parked for later discussion. No implementation has started.

Branch: `codex/mediterranean-word-sea`

## Product idea

A relaxing, highly replayable word-finding mini-game inspired by word-sea games, built specifically for learning Modern Greek.

## Initial direction

- Desktop-first prototype; adapt to a mobile format later.
- Mediterranean sea theme: Greek and Cypriot coast, islands, waves, boats, shells, sunlight, underwater details.
- Greek vocabulary grouped by CEFR level: A2, B1, and later levels.
- Helpful hints so learners do not get stuck.
- Short satisfying rounds with visible progress and gentle rewards.
- Russian or English support follows the selected interface language.
- Calm, non-frightening sound design consistent with the Olympus reward system.

## Questions for the next discussion

- Core mechanic: letters in a circle, word grid, word search, or a combination.
- Whether hints reveal a letter, translation, image, audio, or example sentence.
- Topic packs: travel, food, home, work, sea, Cyprus, exam vocabulary.
- Level progression and session length.
- Relationship to Olympus mascots, gifts, streaks, and subscription access.
- Offline/local progress versus registered cloud progress.

## Guardrail

Keep this mini-game isolated from the existing basic exercises until its mechanics and learning goals are approved.
