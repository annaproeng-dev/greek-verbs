# Modern Greece and Cyprus Themes

Status: product direction saved for later discussion. No interface implementation has started.

Branch: `codex/modern-greece-cyprus-themes`

## Direction

Create thematic learning pages inspired by contemporary everyday life and cultural heritage in Greece and Cyprus. This is a separate visual and reward direction from the Olympus collection.

## Example themes

- Greek coffee and coffee culture.
- Souvlaki, tavernas, bakeries, markets, and local food.
- Cyprus villages, seafronts, mountains, and city life.
- Greek islands, ferries, beaches, and Mediterranean travel.
- Music, crafts, pottery, embroidery, architecture, and festivals.
- Museums, archaeological sites, traditions, and modern cultural life.

## Learning model

- Each theme has its own vocabulary, phrases, images, dialogue, listening, and practice.
- Rewards are modern cultural objects and experiences rather than Greek gods.
- Russian or English explanations follow the selected interface language.
- A2 and B1 variants can reuse a theme while changing vocabulary and task complexity.

## Guardrail

Do not replace or remove the Olympus system in the existing trainer. Treat this as a separate branch and alternative learning world until the user decides how the experiences should coexist.
